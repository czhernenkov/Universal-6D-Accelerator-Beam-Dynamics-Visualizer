Nonlinear studies

Hamiltonian studies automatically disable radiation and stochastic recoil.
Tracking method: canonical paraxial element-map composition with fourth-order split maps for quadrupoles and thick sextupoles/octupoles, thin multipole kicks, and nonlinear RF kicks. The map is intended for Hamiltonian studies; imported sector bends can include thin hard-edge E1/E2 wedge focusing. This is not an Enge fringe-field model; radiation and stochastic recoil are excluded. Imported 3-D field maps can be tracked in a separate relativistic Boris-pusher mode. This is not the canonical symplectic lattice tracker and must be benchmarked for the supplied map, units, boundaries and step size.
References: USPAS Accelerator Physics (Litvinenko, Pozdeyev, Satogata) · MacKay, Thoughts on Healy’s Symplectification Algorithm (BNL C-A/AP/215) · Forest, Symplectic integration (1987) · Yoshida, fourth-order symplectic composition (1990) · Bmad manual · Conte & MacKay, An Introduction to the Physics of Particle Accelerators, 2nd ed. (Chs. 3–6, 7–10; Apps. A and E).
Coordinate convention note: this lattice tracker uses (x, x′, y, y′, z, δ) with z as the code's documented longitudinal path/time-like coordinate and δ=Δp/p₀. Conte & MacKay explicitly warn that longitudinal-coordinate signs, canonical momenta, emittance and multipole numbering differ between references (Appendix A); convert conventions before comparing external maps. The sector-bend 6×6 map follows the ideal hard-edge sector model; edge/fringe details are model-dependent.
